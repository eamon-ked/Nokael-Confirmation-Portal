NOKAEL

Nokael Driver for Android

The driver app for Nokael jobs: your job list, handover codes and live location for dispatch.

Download the app

Android 8 or newer

How to install

  1. Tap “Download the app”If Chrome asks whether to keep the file, tap Download anyway.
  2. Open the downloaded fileTap the notification, or find nokael-driver.apk in your Downloads.
  3. Allow the installAndroid will ask to allow installs from Chrome. Tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back.
  4. Tap InstallIf Play Protect warns about an unknown app, tap More details → Install anyway. The app comes directly from Nokael, not the Play Store.
  5. Open Nokael Driver and sign inEnter your phone number or email and the password dispatch gave you, then allow location so dispatch can see you on jobs.

Updates: the app tells you when a new version is ready. Tap Update, then install it the same way. Your sign-in and jobs are kept.

On an iPhone? Use the web version at coc.nokael.com/driver-app. Trouble installing? Call or WhatsApp dispatch.
